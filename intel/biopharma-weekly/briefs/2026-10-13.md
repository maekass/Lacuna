# What moved biopharma this week — October 13, 2026

Catch-up brief covering **September 19 – October 13, 2026**. The weekly briefs
for September 25, October 2, and October 9 were not produced, so this single
brief replaces them. Three events mattered: the first advisory vote on a
multi-cancer early-detection test, an FDA signal that single-arm Phase 2 data
will not carry an ADC to accelerated approval, and a cluster of breast-cancer
and menopause decisions that sit directly in Lacuna's universe.

Women's-health items are covered in section 3 below rather than in a separate
companion brief. Nothing here is merged into `dataset.verified.json`.

## 1. Galleri clears its AdComm, narrowly

On **September 23** the FDA advisory panel reviewing **GRAIL**'s (NASDAQ:
**GRAL**) **Galleri** premarket approval application voted **10-0 on safety, 6-4
on effectiveness, and 7-2-1 that benefits outweigh risks**
([FDA briefing/vote record](https://www.fda.gov/media/195032/download);
[GRAIL](https://grail.com/press-releases/fda-advisory-committee-votes-in-favor-of-approval-of-grails-galleri-multi-cancer-early-detection-test/)).
It is the first multi-cancer early-detection (MCED) test to reach an FDA panel.

The headline is less useful than the split. Panelists backed Galleri as an
**adjunct to, not a substitute for**, guideline screening, and the narrow
effectiveness vote reflects the absence of a mortality or stage-shift endpoint
([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/fda-advisers-back-safety-grails-cancer-test-narrowly-endorses-efficacy-2026-09-23/)).
The FDA has not set a PMA decision date.

- **Who it affects:** GRAIL; other MCED and liquid-biopsy developers (including
  tracked acquirer `acquirer-exactsciences`); payers. An approval would be the
  first regulatory anchor for MCED coverage decisions.
- **Diagnostics read-through:** an adjunct label means the accuracy bar was met
  but proof of clinical utility was not. Expect payers to price it that way.
- **Watch:** the PMA decision (no date set) and any label language on
  intended-use populations.

## 2. I-DXd pulled: single-arm Phase 2 is no longer enough for ADCs

On **September 25** **Merck** (NYSE: **MRK**) and **Daiichi Sankyo** withdrew
the BLA for **ifinatamab deruxtecan (I-DXd)**, a B7-H3 antibody-drug conjugate,
in extensive-stage small cell lung cancer. The FDA had said the single-arm Phase
2 IDeate-Lung01 data did not support accelerated approval
([BioPharm International](https://www.biopharminternational.com/view/daiichi-sankyo-merck-pull-ifinatamab-deruxtecan-bla-sclc);
[BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/merck-daiichi-fda-withdraw-ifinatamab-deruxtecan-sclc/831440/)).
The program now has to go through the Phase 3 IDeate-Lung02 trial.

- **Who it affects:** Merck/Daiichi; **GSK**, whose rival B7-H3 ADC now leads in
  SCLC; any sponsor planning an accelerated filing on single-arm ORR data in a
  disease with an active standard of care.
- **Read-through:** this is a filing-strategy signal, not a safety one. Model
  later approval dates for single-arm ADC filings, and treat accelerated
  approval as less likely where a randomized trial is already enrolling.

## 3. Breast cancer and menopause: the women's-health cluster

Four items sit inside Lacuna's Breast Health and Menopause sectors.

- **Tukysa frontline maintenance, approved October 7.** The FDA approved
  **Pfizer**'s (NYSE: **PFE**) **tucatinib** with trastuzumab and pertuzumab as
  maintenance therapy for HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer
  ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-tucatinib-trastuzumab-and-pertuzumab-maintenance-treatment-her2-positive-breast-cancer)).
  In HER2CLIMB-05, median PFS was **24.9 vs 16.3 months (HR 0.64)**. The label
  carries a boxed hepatotoxicity warning
  ([Drugs.com](https://www.drugs.com/newdrugs/pfizer-s-tukysa-regimen-receives-fda-approval-front-line-maintenance-her2-metastatic-breast-cancer-6909.html)).
  Lacuna: tracked acquirer `acquirer-pfizer`.
- **Lynkuet (elinzanetant) sNDA, priority review accepted September 28.**
  **Bayer**'s NK-1/NK-3 antagonist is filed for vasomotor symptoms caused by
  endocrine therapy for HR+ breast cancer (OASIS-4). Bayer did not disclose an
  action date
  ([Bayer](https://www.bayer.com/en-us/united-states/news-and-stories/2026/fda-accepts-snda)).
  It would be the first US-approved therapy for this indication. The watchlist's
  **Q2-2027 date is an estimate** (`date_basis=estimated`), not sponsor
  guidance. Lacuna: `acquirer-bayer`, Menopause.
- **Giredestrant: two Roche PDUFAs coming.** One is **November 30** for adjuvant
  ER+/HER2- early breast cancer (lidERA, iDFS HR 0.70, priority review). The
  other is **December 18** with everolimus for ESR1-mutated advanced disease
  (evERA)
  ([OncLive](https://www.onclive.com/view/fda-flashback-breast-cancer-decisions-and-news-from-august-2026)).
  An adjuvant approval would make it the first oral SERD in the curative
  setting. Lacuna: `acquirer-roche`, Breast Health.
- **FDA testosterone-for-women workshop (September 17).** This was a listening
  session, not a regulatory action, on an indication with no FDA-approved
  product for women ([FDA Law Blog](https://www.thefdalawblog.com/)). It marks a
  policy direction, not a catalyst.

- **Vertex inaxaplin, Sep 22:** Phase 2b AMPLIFIED cut UACR by **42.7%** in the

  The AMPLITUDE interim analysis is guided for early 2027.
- **VRBPAC, Oct 1:** voted 9-0 for the 2027 Southern Hemisphere flu strains
