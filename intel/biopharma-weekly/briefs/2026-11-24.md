# What moved biopharma this week — November 24, 2026

Window: Nov 17–24, 2026. Catch-up note: scheduled runs for Oct 23 through Nov 20

[Oct 16 brief](2026-10-16.md).

## The 3 events that mattered most

**1. FDA rejects Capricor's deramiocel a second time, citing data integrity (Nov
20).** Capricor (CAPR) received a second CRL for its allogeneic cell therapy in
Duchenne muscular dystrophy. In an unusual
[public FDA statement](https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/whats-new-biologics/statement-deramiocel-bla-duchenne-muscular-dystrophy),
the agency cited BIMO-identified violations in the pivotal HOPE-3 trial,
including unauthorized post hoc statistical-analysis-plan changes and blinding
breaches. FDA said these "cannot be remediated post-hoc", and it recommended a
new randomized trial
([Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/capricor-weathers-second-dmd-cell-therapy-snub-fda-alleges-serious-data-integrity-concerns)).
Shares fell 76% to $1.80, and the company is weighing several hundred job cuts
while saying it will appeal
([Reuters via Sacramento Bee](https://www.sacbee.com/news/business/article318210538.html)).
Downstream impact: this shifts rare-disease cell and gene therapy risk from "is
the effect clinically meaningful" to "was the trial run cleanly." Expect closer
diligence on SAP version control and blinding for sponsors relying on a single
pivotal study. In the same window, FDA said it would miss Savara's (SVRA) Nov 22
MOLBREEVI date with no new date set
([BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/fda/fda-will-miss-rare-decision-deadline-for-savara-candidate-after-three-month-delay)),
so assume some PDUFA dates slip without warning.

**2. Roche's giredestrant becomes the first oral SERD in early breast cancer
(Nov 23).** FDA approved Wilserdly (giredestrant) as adjuvant therapy for
ER+/HER2- early breast cancer. FDA called it the
[first new hormone therapy for early-stage breast cancer in more than 20 years](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-new-hormone-therapy-early-stage-breast-cancer-more-20-years).
The decision came a week ahead of its Nov 30 goal and one week after the Nov 16
approval with everolimus in ESR1-mutated advanced disease
([Genentech via BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/fda-approves-genentechs-wilserdly-giredestrant-a-next-generation-oral-serd-for-er-positive-esr1-mutated-advanced-breast-cancer)).
Roche (RHHBY) now holds the only oral SERD label spanning early and advanced
disease. That raises the bar for AstraZeneca's camizestrant (AZN), Lilly's
Inluriyo (LLY) and Pfizer/Arvinas's vepdegestrant (PFE/ARVN) in the much larger
adjuvant market. Lilly presents EMBER-3 final OS as a SABCS late-breaker on Dec
11
([Lilly](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-highlights-strength-breast-cancer-portfolio-new-data)).
Women's-health relevance: high (Breast Health; `acquirer-roche`).

**3. A run of early rare-disease and oncology approvals.** BridgeBio's (BBIO)
Welgemda (BBP-418) is the first approved therapy for any limb-girdle muscular
dystrophy. It received traditional approval plus a priority review voucher on
Nov 23, four days early
([BridgeBio 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1743881/000174388126000050/bbiowelgemdapdufa-ex991.htm)).
Cogent's (COGT) Ekitna (bezuclastinib) plus sunitinib was approved in
post-imatinib GIST on Nov 24, six days early. PEAK showed median PFS of 16.5 vs
9.2 months
([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-bezuclastinib-combination-sunitinib-advanced-gastrointestinal-stromal-tumors)).
CDER's novel-approval list also added Nuvalent's Nelkarmi (ALK+ NSCLC), Sanofi's
Infilbray (type 3 Gaucher), Regeneron's Livsayis (gMG) and Lembaz (Niemann-Pick
C) during Nov 17–18
([FDA novel approvals 2026](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/novel-drug-approvals-fda/novel-drug-approvals-2026)).
Implication: approvals are arriving early even while reviewers miss other dates,
so early-action scenarios belong in near-term models. Cogent's NonAdvSM PDUFA on
Dec 30 is next.

**Also this week.** SELLAS's (SLS) REGAL Phase 3 in AML showed an OS HR of 0.59
([RTTNews](https://www.rttnews.com/3715565/sellas-life-sciences-shares-soar-175-on-positive-phase-3-cancer-trial-results.aspx)).
Novartis's (NVS) ORION-4 gave Leqvio its first positive CV-outcomes readout; no
HR has been disclosed yet
([Novartis](https://prod1.novartis.com/news/media-releases/twice-yearly-leqvio-inclisiran-reduced-cardiovascular-event-risk-first-outcomes-study-sirna-therapy-ascvd)).
FDA held a general-issues Microbiology Devices Panel on Nov 19 with no vote
([FDA summary](https://www.fda.gov/media/196997/download)). No new drug AdComms
appear on the
[FDA calendar](https://www.fda.gov/advisory-committees/advisory-committee-calendar).
Since the last brief, Summit's (SMMT) ivonescimab received a CRL on Nov 13
because HARMONi showed no OS benefit and its data were China-heavy
([Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/pharma/fdas-complete-response-letter-summit-ivonescimab-cites-china-heavy-data-beyond-survival-miss)).
That sets a precedent for PD-1×VEGF bispecifics.
