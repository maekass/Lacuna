# What moved biopharma this week — September 18, 2026

Week of September 12–18, 2026. Three events mattered: a first-ever approval in a
disease that had none, a European precedent that lowers the cost of building a
biosimilar, and a two-year dataset that turns an accelerated approval into a
likely full one.

Women's-health items — CHMP opinions in ovarian and HER2+ breast cancer, and the
MIUDELLA duration sNDA — are mapped onto Lacuna surfaces in the companion
[2026-09-18-womens-health.md](./2026-09-18-womens-health.md). Nothing in that
brief is merged into `dataset.verified.json`.

## 1. Sanfilippo A goes from untreatable to treated

On **September 17** the FDA granted full approval to **FAYUVI** (**rebisufligene
etisparvovec-hopf**, formerly UX111) from **Ultragenyx** (NASDAQ: **RARE**), for
the neurologic manifestations of mucopolysaccharidosis type IIIA — Sanfilippo
syndrome Type A — in pediatric patients with preserved neurodevelopmental
function
([Ultragenyx](https://ir.ultragenyx.com/news-releases/news-release-details/ultragenyx-announces-approval-fayuvitm-gene-therapy-first-ever)).
It is the **first-ever FDA-approved treatment for the disease**, Ultragenyx's
second gene therapy and sixth approval overall, and it came with a priority
review voucher
([Fierce Pharma](https://www.fiercepharma.com/pharma/fda-approves-ultragenyx-gene-therapy-fayuvi-rare-neurodegenerative-disorder)).

Two details carry more signal than the headline. First, the approval landed
**two days before the September 19 goal date**, and it followed a **prior
complete response letter** on the same program — a reminder that a CRL on
chemistry, manufacturing and controls is a schedule event, not a verdict on the
asset. Second, the EU is moving the other way on a sibling program: at this
week's CHMP, **Scholar Rock withdrew its European application for apitegromab**
(Isembyldt) entirely
([EMA](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/meeting-highlights-committee-medicinal-products-human-use-chmp-14-17-september-2026))
— the same molecule the **FDA approved on September 11** as **Isembyld**
([Scholar Rock](https://investors.scholarrock.com/news-releases/news-release-details/scholar-rock-announces-fda-approval-isembyldtm-apitegromab-mstn)).
Same dossier, opposite outcomes, seven days apart. Transatlantic filing
divergence is becoming a routine rather than exceptional risk in rare disease.

## 2. Europe approved a biosimilar without a comparative efficacy trial

The CHMP recommended **Pebrilzo** (**pertuzumab**, Biosimilar Collaborations
Ireland) for HER2-positive early and metastatic breast cancer
([EMA](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/pebrilzo)), and stated
that it is _"the first biosimilar medicine recommended for approval following
the tailored clinical approach"_
([EMA meeting highlights](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/meeting-highlights-committee-medicinal-products-human-use-chmp-14-17-september-2026)).

That approach comes from the reflection paper EMA finalised **16 March 2026**,
under which **comparative efficacy studies may not be required** for
well-characterised biological substances when analytical comparability, plus PK
and appropriate safety data, provide a more sensitive test of biosimilarity
([EMA reflection paper](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/reflection-paper-tailored-clinical-approach-biosimilar-development),
[RAPS](https://www.raps.org/resource/euro-roundup-ema-finalizes-reflection-paper-on-biosimilar-comparative-efficacy-studies.html)).
This week is the first time that pathway produced an actual positive opinion.
The Phase 3 equivalence trial has been the single largest line item in
biosimilar development; removing it compresses both cost and time to market, and
it does so first in **breast cancer** — the most crowded biosimilar battleground
there is.

**Read the "first" carefully.** Pebrilzo is _not_ Europe's first pertuzumab
biosimilar: Henlius and Organon's **POHERDY** took that title with EC approval
on **29 April 2026**
([Organon](https://www.organon.com/news/european-commission-ec-approves-henlius-and-organons-poherdy-pertuzumab-the-first-approved-biosimilar-to-perjeta-pertuzumab-in-europe/)).
The precedent is regulatory, not molecular.

## 3. Trutakna's two-year data leaves little room for argument

**Vera Therapeutics** (NASDAQ: **VERA**) reported the final 104-week efficacy
analysis of **ORIGIN 3** for **TRUTAKNA** (**atacicept-vymj**) in IgA
nephropathy on **September 15**
([Vera Therapeutics](https://ir.veratx.com/news-releases/news-release-details/vera-therapeutics-announces-trutaknatm-atacicept-vymj-stabilized)):

| Endpoint (104 weeks)                        | Trutakna                                                   | Placebo           |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Composite kidney-disease progression events | 11                                                         | 38                |
| Hazard ratio                                | 0.24 (95% CI 0.12–0.48), p<0.0001 — **76% risk reduction** | —                 |
| Annualized eGFR slope (mL/min/1.73m²/yr)    | −0.6 (95% CI −1.6, 0.4)                                    | −5.6 (−6.6, −4.6) |
| Dialysis ≥30 days, transplant or death      | 0                                                          | 8                 |

An eGFR slope of −0.6/year is close to the decline seen in healthy aging; the
placebo arm lost nearly ten times as much. Vera plans a **supplemental BLA for
full approval in Q4 2026**, converting the accelerated approval it received in
July
([STAT](https://www.statnews.com/2026/09/15/vera-kidney-drug-trutakna-shows-strong-phase-3-results-igan/)).
Zero hard kidney events versus eight is the kind of result that sets the bar
competitors in IgAN now have to clear.

## Also this week

- **Women's health ran through the entire CHMP docket.** Beyond Pebrilzo, the
  committee backed **Lifyorli** (**relacorilant**, Corcept Therapeutics, NASDAQ:
  **CORT**) with nab-paclitaxel in platinum-resistant high-grade epithelial
  ovarian, fallopian tube or primary peritoneal cancer after one to three prior
  regimens, and **Sepalna** (**senaparib**) as maintenance in advanced
  high-grade ovarian cancer
  ([EMA](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/meeting-highlights-committee-medicinal-products-human-use-chmp-14-17-september-2026)).
  Relacorilant's cortisol-modulation mechanism is orthogonal to PARP inhibition
  and anti-angiogenics, so it widens the platinum-resistant option set;
  senaparib adds a further PARP inhibitor to a segment where EU pricing pressure
  already outweighs mechanism novelty.
- **Zipalertinib bought six months of PFS at a real tolerability price.**
  **REZILIENT3** (Cullinan Therapeutics, NASDAQ: **CGEM**, with Taiho) hit its
  primary endpoint in first-line EGFR exon 20 insertion NSCLC: median PFS **14.5
  months vs 8.5**, HR **0.50** (95% CI 0.34–0.73), p=0.00015 by blinded central
  review. Grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events were **80.7% vs 40.4%**,
  with **three treatment-related deaths (2.1%)** versus none on chemotherapy
  alone
  ([WCLC coverage](https://lungsummit.org/articles/wclc26-rezilient-3-zipalertinib-exon20/),
  [Cullinan presentation](https://cullinantherapeutics.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/REZILIENT3-WCLC2026-Presentation_13SEP2026.Final_.pdf)).
  Doubling severe toxicity in a first-line setting is a commercial constraint,
  not a footnote.
- **The hormone-free IUD is going for six years.** The FDA accepted Sebela
  Women's Health's sNDA to extend **MIUDELLA** (copper intrauterine system,
  commercialised by **Organon**, NYSE: **OGN**) from three years of labelled use
  to six, announced September 14, with action expected in **Q2 calendar 2027**
  ([Organon](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260914839018/en/)).
  Doubling labelled duration roughly halves the per-year cost of the only
  hormone-free IUD approved in the US.
- **The CHMP recommended 12 new medicines and 10 label extensions with zero
  negative opinions**, including **Tecvayli** (teclistamab) monotherapy moving
  to patients with at least one prior line of myeloma therapy, and **Frehemgo**
  (**denecimig**, Novo Nordisk) for haemophilia A prophylaxis — the same
  molecule whose US goal date falls on **September 30**
  ([EMA](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/meeting-highlights-committee-medicinal-products-human-use-chmp-14-17-september-2026),
  [EMA — Tecvayli variation](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/variation/tecvayli)).
- **A new advisory committee was scheduled, and it is a framework session.** The
  **Microbiology Devices Panel** will meet **November 19** on pathogen-agnostic
  sequencing for outbreak preparedness — test design, development challenges,
  and how to validate bioinformatics that must absorb constant sequence change.
  No sponsor, no named device
  ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/advisory-committees/advisory-committee-calendar/november-19-2026-microbiology-devices-panel-medical-devices-advisory-committee-meeting-announcement)).

## Data-integrity notes

- **Last week's open question closed, and the caution was justified.** Telix's
  **Pixclara** (floretyrosine F 18) PDUFA passed on September 11 with no public
  decision, and last week's brief deliberately left the row `upcoming`. The FDA
  novel-approvals list now dates the approval **9/11/2026** (entry #40) while
  Telix announced it on **September 14**
  ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/novel-drug-approvals-fda/novel-drug-approvals-2026),
  [Telix](https://telixpharma.com/news-views/fda-approves-telixs-brain-cancer-imaging-drug-pixclara/)).
  It is the first FDA-approved FET-PET imaging drug for glioma. The three-day
  disclosure lag, not agency silence, was the signal.
- **A wrong indication has been corrected in the watchlist.** The September 26
  PDUFA row for **zilurgisertib** read _anemia of myelofibrosis_, seeded from a
  pdufa.bio listing. The NDA is for **fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva** in
  patients 12 and older, under Priority Review, with **Mirum** (NASDAQ:
  **MIRM**) holding worldwide rights licensed from **Incyte**
  ([Incyte](https://investor.incyte.com/news-releases/news-release-details/mirum-pharmaceuticals-and-incyte-announce-positive-pivotal-phase/)).
  This is the third aggregator-sourced error caught in this file. Calendar sites
  are acceptable for _dates_; they are not acceptable for indications.
- **Two aggregators were stale or wrong this week.** pdufa.bio's October page
  (last updated August 20) still lists an **October 24** decision for
  **leniolisib**, which the FDA already approved on September 11
  ([pdufa.bio](https://www.pdufa.bio/calendar/2026/october)). A secondary
  summary of the CHMP meeting listed **11** label expansions including
  **Pradaxa**, where EMA's own highlights list **10** and do not include it
  ([AllSci](https://allsci.com/news/tecvayli-chmp-opinion-recommends-johnson-johnsons/)).
