# What moved biopharma this week — August 28, 2026

## The three events that mattered

### 1. First targeted therapy for pancreatic cancer: FDA approves Rasonque

On August 26, the FDA approved Revolution Medicines' (RVMD) Rasonque
(daraxonrasib), a RAS(ON) multi-selective inhibitor, for adults with metastatic
pancreatic adenocarcinoma after at least one prior systemic therapy — the first
targeted therapy ever approved for the most common form of pancreatic cancer
([FDA oncology approvals](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/oncology-cancerhematologic-malignancies-approval-notifications),
[RAPS](https://www.raps.org/resource/this-week-at-fda-white-house-considering-new-fda-posts-fda-approves-slew-of-firsts-and-more.html)).
Reuters reports the drug roughly doubled survival in advanced disease in its
pivotal trial
([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/regulatory/)).
Drug classes affected: RAS-targeted therapies broadly — this validates direct
RAS(ON) inhibition beyond KRAS G12C and raises the bar for competitors (Lilly's
olomorasib just took Breakthrough designation in KRAS G12C pancreatic cancer,
[Chemxplore](https://chemxplore.com/news/regulatory-and-compliance-updates)).
Downstream: watch RVMD's first-line RASolute readouts and read-through to the
entire RAS/MAPK pipeline.

### 2. A sweep of firsts — and another early approval

FDA cleared four more notable products this week: Lisraya (brepocitinib,
Priovant/Roivant ROIV), the first oral treatment for dermatomyositis, on August
27 — more than a month ahead of its September 30 PDUFA date
([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/news-events-human-drugs/whats-new-related-drugs));
Imaavy (nipocalimab, J&J JNJ), the first drug for warm autoimmune hemolytic
anemia, August 25
([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/news-events-human-drugs/notable-approvals-drugs));
Ziihera (zanidatamab, Jazz JAZZ/Zymeworks ZYME) with and without BeOne's
tislelizumab for first-line HER2+ gastroesophageal adenocarcinoma, August 25,
establishing a new front-line standard of care with median OS of 26.4 months
([Jazz](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/us-fda-approves-ziihera-zanidatamab-hrii-with-and-without-tislelizumab-plus-chemotherapy-in-first-line-her2-advanced-gastroesophageal-adenocarcinoma-302859470.html),
[breakoutbiotechstocks](https://www.breakoutbiotechstocks.com/)); and Bixlenvo
(bictegravir/lenacapavir, Gilead GILD), the smallest once-daily single-tablet
HIV regimen for virologically suppressed adults, August 27
([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/us-fda-approves-gileads-once-daily-combination-pill-hiv-treatment-2026-08-27/)).
Lilly's (LLY) Mounjaro also added a cardiovascular risk-reduction indication on
SURPASS-CVOT data August 28
([MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/stocks/NYSE/LLY/fda-events/)). Drug
classes: TYK2/JAK inhibitors, FcRn blockers (nipocalimab lands squarely in
argenx's territory a week after its ALKIVIA myositis win), HER2 bispecifics,
capsid inhibitors, incretins. Downstream: the early Lisraya action continues
August's pattern of ahead-of-schedule approvals and sharpens the myositis
showdown with argenx's efgartigimod.

### 3. Capricor's DMD decision punted three months

On August 24, Capricor (CAPR) said FDA extended the PDUFA date for deramiocel,
its cell therapy for Duchenne muscular dystrophy cardiomyopathy, from August 22
to November 22, 2026, after accepting new 24-month HOPE-3 extension data and a
refined upper-limb-function indication as a major amendment
([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/us-fda-extends-review-capricors-muscle-disorder-therapy-by-three-months-2026-08-24/),
[Capricor 8-K](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/CAPR/8-k-capricor-therapeutics-inc-reports-material-event-b9849b36815b.html)).
Drug class: allogeneic cardiosphere-derived cell therapy. Downstream: after
July's 9–3 negative AdComm, the extension — rather than a second CRL — suggests
FDA is at least willing to weigh the amended indication; the November date is
now one of Q4's highest-stakes binary events for cell therapy in DMD.

## Also notable

- Cytokinetics (CYTK) presented full ACACIA-HCM Phase 3 results at ESC
  (published in NEJM): aficamten met both dual primary endpoints in
  non-obstructive HCM (KCCQ-CSS p=0.021; peak VO2 p=0.003), with an sNDA planned
  for Q4 2026 — a first for the myosin-inhibitor class in nHCM
  ([Cytokinetics](https://ir.cytokinetics.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2026/Cytokinetics-Announces-Positive-Results-from-ACACIA-HCM-Presented-in-Hot-Line-Session-at-the-European-Society-of-Cardiology-ESC-Congress-2026-and-Published-in-The-New-England-Journal-of-Medicine/default.aspx)).
- Leqembi IQLIK (lecanemab SC starting dose) was approved back on July 13; US
  launch is expected in late August via specialty pharmacy — our watchlist date
  reflected the original PDUFA
  ([Eisai/Biogen](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/fda-approves-leqembi-iqlik-lecanemab-irmb-subcutaneous-injection-as-an-initiation-dose-for-early-alzheimers-disease-302824254.html)).
- CHMP's August written procedure (Aug 17–20) produced no new opinions; the next
  full meeting is September 14–17
  ([EMA](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/events/committee-medicinal-products-human-use-chmp-17-20-august-2026)).

  October 2
