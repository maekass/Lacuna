# What moved biopharma this week — September 4, 2026

## The three events that mattered

### 1. Two first-of-their-kind myeloproliferative neoplasm approvals in four days

The FDA approved **Mimrylo (rusfertide)** on August 28 for adults with
polycythemia vera — the first hepcidin-mimetic peptide ever approved, and the
first PV therapy that works by restricting iron availability rather than
suppressing marrow output
([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-drug-its-kind-polycythemia-vera-rare-blood-disorder),
[Takeda](https://www.takeda.com/newsroom/newsreleases/2026/fda-approval-mimrylo/)).
It is partnered between **Protagonist Therapeutics (PTGX)**, which discovered
it, and **Takeda (TAK)**, which will commercialize it; the approval arrived
roughly a month ahead of the September 30 PDUFA date and was supported by the
Phase 3 VERIFY trial
([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/us-fda-approves-takeda-protagonists-drug-rare-blood-cancer-2026-08-28/)).
BioSpace frames it as a ~$2B opportunity and Takeda's second of three critical
near-term launches to clear the FDA
([BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/fda/takeda-protagonist-score-broad-fda-nod-in-rare-blood-cancer-setting-up-2b-opportunity)).

Three days later, on August 31, **PharmaEssentia's Besremi (ropeginterferon
alfa-2b-njft)** was approved for essential thrombocythemia — the first new agent
approved for ET in the United States since anagrelide in 1997, cleared
regardless of genotype or disease status on Phase 3 SURPASS-ET data
([FDA notable approvals](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/news-events-human-drugs/notable-approvals-drugs),
[PharmaEssentia](https://us.pharmaessentia.com/category/press-release/),
[OncLive](https://www.onclive.com/view/fda-approves-ropeginterferon-alfa-2b-for-essential-thrombocythemia)).

Drug classes affected: hepcidin mimetics (validated as a class for the first
time), monopegylated interferons, and by extension the JAK inhibitors that have
anchored MPN treatment for a decade. Downstream: two of the three classic
Philadelphia-negative MPNs got new mechanisms in one week, and
cytoreduction-sparing approaches now have label support in both. Watch Incyte's
zilurgisertib PDUFA on September 26 in anemia of myelofibrosis — the third MPN
decision of the quarter.

### 2. Antisense oligonucleotides split: a first-ever approval and a Phase 3 failure, 24 hours apart

On September 3 the FDA approved **Zanvastro (zilganersen)** from **Ionis
(IONS)** as the first and only disease-modifying treatment for Alexander disease
in pediatric and adult patients — the first therapy to directly target the GFAP
protein buildup that drives the disease
([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-drug-treat-alexander-disease),
[Ionis](https://ir.ionis.com/news-releases/news-release-details/zanvastrotm-zilganersen-approved-fda-first-and-only-disease)).
Ionis received a Priority Review Voucher alongside the approval, and Zanvastro
is the company's first independent neurology launch; the decision landed about
three weeks before its September 22 PDUFA date
([pdufa.bio calendar](https://www.pdufa.bio/calendar/2026/september),
[FDA novel approvals 2026](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/novel-drug-approvals-fda/novel-drug-approvals-2026)).

The day before, **Ultragenyx (RARE)** reported that Phase 3 Aspire missed on
**apazunersen (GTX-102)** in Angelman syndrome — failing both the primary
endpoint (change from baseline in Bayley-4 cognitive raw score) and the key
secondary endpoint (net response on the Multidomain Responder Index)
([Ultragenyx](https://ir.ultragenyx.com/news-releases/news-release-details/ultragenyx-announces-phase-3-aspire-results-angelman-syndrome),
[Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4639823-ultragenyx-drops-on-phase-3-trial-failure-for-angelman-syndrome-drug)).
The company said there were no differences between treated and control patients
that would support the program as designed, and is now weighing significant cost
cuts
([MedCity News](https://medcitynews.com/2026/09/ultragenyx-angelman-syndrome-apazunersen-rare-disease-antisense-oligonucleotide-aso/),
[BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/topic/clinical-trials/)).

Drug class: intrathecally delivered ASOs for rare CNS disease. Downstream: the
modality is not the variable — target biology is. Reducing a toxic
gain-of-function protein (GFAP) worked; de-repressing a silenced paternal allele
to restore cognition did not. RARE now carries a bruised balance sheet into its
own **UX111 PDUFA on September 19**, two weeks out.

### 3. Autoimmune CAR-T hits a safety wall — at two companies at once

**Novartis (NVS)** paused eight clinical trials of **rapcabtagene autoleucel
(rap-cel)**, its autologous CD19 CAR-T for rheumatic and neurological autoimmune
disease, after three patient deaths involving a severe, potentially
life-threatening immune reaction; the halt was implemented in late August and
its oncology trials are unaffected
([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/novartis-pauses-autoimmune-cell-therapy-studies-after-deaths-wsj-reports-2026-09-01/),
[WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/health/pharma/novartis-pauses-autoimmune-cell-therapy-studies-after-deaths-62113d35),
[Healio](https://www.healio.com/news/rheumatology/20260902/three-deaths-halt-novartis-car-tcell-trials-in-autoimmune-diseases)).
Fierce Biotech reported that **Bristol Myers Squibb** halted a comparable
autoimmune CAR-T program over immune-related adverse events in the same window
([Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/novartis-bristol-myers-squibb-halt-car-t-cell-trials-due-immune-events)).

Drug class: autologous CD19-directed CAR-T outside oncology. Downstream: the
entire investment case for cell therapy in autoimmune disease rests on accepting
oncology-grade toxicity in non-fatal indications. Two independent programs
pausing in the same week resets that risk calculus and shifts near-term
advantage to in-vivo CAR platforms and to T-cell engagers, which are dosed and
stopped rather than infused once.

## Also notable

- **Novartis remibrutinib** met the primary endpoint in both Phase 3 REMODEL-1
  and REMODEL-2 in relapsing MS, beating teriflunomide on annualized relapse
  rate and all key secondary endpoints including disability progression — with
  no signal of the liver toxicity that has dogged other BTK inhibitors. Filing
  and full data are planned around October
  ([Novartis](https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-remibrutinib-high-efficacy-oral-btk-inhibitor-significantly-reduces-relapse-rates-and-shows-favorable-safety-profile-phase-iii-rms-trials),
  [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/novartis-oral-multiple-sclerosis-drug-meets-main-goal-late-stage-studies-2026-09-01/)).
- **Alumis (ALMS)** missed both primary and key secondary endpoints with
  **envudeucitinib** in the Phase 2b LUMUS lupus trial, though a high-interferon
  subgroup showed activity; the company still intends to advance in that subset
  ([Alumis](https://investors.alumis.com/news-releases/news-release-details/alumis-announces-topline-results-envudeucitinib-phase-2b-trial/),
  [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/alumis-eyes-late-stage-lupus-study-despite-trial-miss-2026-09-01/)).
  Another TYK2 inhibitor struggling outside psoriasis.
- **Summit (SMMT)/Akeso** reported a statistically significant overall survival
  benefit for **ivonescimab** monotherapy versus pembrolizumab monotherapy in
  PD-L1-positive advanced NSCLC in Akeso's China-based HARMONi-2 study — the
  first time a PD-1/VEGF bispecific has beaten Keytruda on OS
  ([Summit](https://smmttx.com/news/press-releases/news-details/2026/Ivonescimab-Monotherapy-Demonstrates-Statistically-Significant-Overall-Survival-Benefit-Compared-to-Pembrolizumab-Monotherapy-in-PD-L1-Positive-Advanced-NSCLC-in-HARMONi-2-Study-Conducted-by-Akeso-in-China/default.aspx)).
- **AbbVie (ABBV)** closed its **$10.9B acquisition of Apogee Therapeutics** on
  September 3 at $135.11 per share in cash, and the same morning reported
  positive Phase 3 CERVINO results for etentamig in relapsed/refractory multiple
  myeloma
  ([AbbVie](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/abbvie-completes-acquisition-of-apogee-therapeutics-302868995.html),
  [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/health-latest-news/clinical-trials-medical-discoveries-list/)).
  **Eli Lilly (LLY)** added a $2.875B agreement for Merida Biosciences
  ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/eli-lilly-continues-near-monthly-111513059.html)).
- **Safety:** FDA added a boxed warning to ferric carboxymaltose (Injectafer)
  labeling on September 1 for severe, prolonged symptomatic hypophosphatemia,
  following a granted citizen petition
  ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-safety-communications/fda-adds-boxed-warning-labeling-ferric-carboxymaltose-injection-injectafer-describe-risk-low),
  [FDA What's New](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/news-events-human-drugs/whats-new-related-drugs)).
  Relevant to obstetric and gynecologic iron-deficiency practice, where IV iron
  use has grown quickly.
