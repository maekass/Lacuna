# What moved biopharma this week — August 21, 2026

## The three events that mattered

### 1. Moderna's individualized mRNA cancer therapy clears Phase 3 in melanoma

At an interim analysis on August 19, intismeran autogene (mRNA-4157) plus
Merck's Keytruda met both recurrence-free and distant metastasis-free survival
endpoints in the Phase 3 INTerpath-001 trial in resected high-risk melanoma —
the first positive Phase 3 for an individualized mRNA cancer treatment, sending
Moderna (MRNA) shares surging and lifting BioNTech (BNTX) in sympathy
([BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/topic/clinical-trials/),
[Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/moderna-rallies-8-biontech-climbs-140607167.html)).
Regulatory filings are planned. Drug classes affected: individualized neoantigen
therapies (validation for the whole class, including BioNTech and CureVac
programs) and PD-1 checkpoint inhibitors (Merck, MRK, extends the Keytruda
franchise). Downstream: watch read-through to BNTX's iNeST programs and renewed
platform credibility for mRNA oncology after a bruising two years for the
sector.

### 2. FDA clears two rare-disease firsts — both ahead of schedule

On August 19 the FDA granted accelerated approval to Ultragenyx's (RARE)
GENGLYCOS (pariglasgene brecaparvovec, DTX401), the first gene therapy for
glycogen storage disease type Ia, four days before its August 23 PDUFA date
([Ultragenyx IR](https://ir.ultragenyx.com/news-releases/news-release-details/ultragenyx-announces-us-fda-approval-genglycostm-gene-therapy)).
The same day, FDA approved Regeneron's (REGN) Pasatru (garetosmab), the
second-ever therapy for fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva — nearly two weeks
ahead of its August 31 PDUFA date
([FDA novel approvals](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/novel-drug-approvals-fda/novel-drug-approvals-2026),
[FDA notable approvals](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/news-events-human-drugs/notable-approvals-drugs)).
Drug classes: AAV gene therapy and activin-A antibodies. Downstream: early
actions continue a pattern (Zenbexus/iberdomide also cleared four days early on
August 13, the first CELMoD,
[FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-iberdomide-daratumumab-and-hyaluronidase-fihj-and-dexamethasone))
— a supportive signal for the crowded late-August/September rare-disease PDUFA
slate (UX111, zilganersen, brepocitinib).

### 3. argenx expands the FcRn franchise with a myositis Phase 3 win

On August 17, argenx (ARGX) reported that Vyvgart Hytrulo (efgartigimod) met the
primary endpoint of the Phase 3 ALKIVIA trial in autoimmune myositis (p=0.0011),
the first Phase 3 to show a significant benefit in immune-mediated necrotizing
myopathy, a subtype with no approved therapy; shares jumped ~17%
([argenx press release](https://www.stockwatch.com/News/Item/U-z1001257826-U!ARGX-20260816/U/ARGX),
[Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/argenx-jumps-phase-3-data-121200604.html)).
Drug class: FcRn antagonists. Downstream: strengthens ARGX's case ahead of
competing FcRn and complement programs in myositis, and sets up a direct label
race with Priovant/Roivant's brepocitinib (TYK2/JAK1), whose dermatomyositis
PDUFA lands September 30
([CheckRare](https://checkrare.com/2026-orphan-drugs-pdufa-dates-and-fda-approvals/)).
