# What moved biopharma this week — October 16, 2026

Week of October 10–16, 2026. Three events mattered:

- An EU recommendation that puts an oral GLP-1 and a first RAS(ON) inhibitor on
  track for the European market.
- Two FDA approvals that accepted lighter evidence than usual, one week after
  the agency refused to accept single-arm data for an ADC.
- A third late-stage miss for BioNTech's mRNA cancer immunotherapies.

Women's-health items are in section 4. Nothing here is merged into
`dataset.verified.json`.

## 1. CHMP backs an oral GLP-1 and a pan-RAS pancreatic drug

At its **October 12–15** meeting, EMA's CHMP recommended **six new medicines**
and **five indication extensions**, with no negative opinions on new medicines
([EMA](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/meeting-highlights-committee-medicinal-products-human-use-chmp-12-15-october-2026)).
Two of the opinions define new classes in Europe:

- **Foundayo (orforglipron)**, **Eli Lilly** (NYSE: **LLY**). A once-daily oral
  GLP-1 receptor agonist tablet for obesity and type 2 diabetes in adults
  ([EMA](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/meeting-highlights-committee-medicinal-products-human-use-chmp-12-15-october-2026)).
  It is a small molecule, so its manufacturing does not face the peptide
  capacity limits of injectable incretins.
- **Rasonque (daraxonrasib)**, **Revolution Medicines** (NASDAQ: **RVMD**). For
  metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma after at least one prior therapy,
  reviewed in phases under EMA's Cancer Medicines Pathfinder
  ([EMA](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/meeting-highlights-committee-medicinal-products-human-use-chmp-12-15-october-2026)).
  The opinion rests on RASolute 302, which met all primary and key secondary
  endpoints in both the RAS G12-mutant and intent-to-treat populations
  ([Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4653801-lilly-pfizer-jj-eu-drug-recommendations-this-month)).

Other opinions:

- **Extensions:** Tukysa (see section 4), Talvey in second-line combination for
  relapsed/refractory myeloma
  ([EMA](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/variation/talvey)), and
  Tryngolza.
- **Biosimilars:** two positive opinions, Fovlussea (ranibizumab) and Neuzafy
  (filgrastim).
- **Refusal:** on re-examination, the CHMP upheld its refusal of **Yartemlea
  (narsoplimab)**, which the FDA approved in January
  ([EMA](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/meeting-highlights-committee-medicinal-products-human-use-chmp-12-15-october-2026);
  [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/news-events-human-drugs/notable-approvals-drugs)).

What follows:

- **Who it affects:** Lilly, as the oral alternative to injectable semaglutide
  and tirzepatide in the EU. RVMD and other RAS-pathway developers. Novo
  Nordisk's oral franchise faces direct competition.
- **Read-through:** European Commission decisions usually follow within about
  two months. Watch EU pricing and reimbursement for Foundayo. National health
  technology assessment bodies, not the CHMP, decide uptake for obesity drugs.

## 2. FDA flexibility: two approvals on lighter evidence

**Anktiva, October 14.** The FDA expanded **ImmunityBio**'s (NASDAQ: **IBRX**)
**Anktiva** plus BCG to **BCG-unresponsive, high-grade papillary-only NMIBC**.
It **extrapolated** efficacy from the carcinoma in situ (CIS) cohort (n=77, 62%
complete response) rather than requiring a separate trial
([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-treatment-certain-people-bcg-unresponsive-bladder-cancer)).
The FDA called this "a new FDA approach" and said it is finalizing guidance that
would let CIS evidence support papillary-only approvals
([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-treatment-certain-people-bcg-unresponsive-bladder-cancer)).
The approval came almost three months before the January 6, 2027 action date.
The FDA had refused to file this papillary application in May 2025
([BioMed Nexus](https://updates.biomednexus.com/p/the-fda-approved-what-it-refused-to-file-17-months-ago)).

**Enspryng, October 15.** The FDA approved **Roche**'s (OTCQX: **RHHBY**)
**Enspryng (satralizumab)** for **thyroid eye disease**. It is the first at-home
subcutaneous TED therapy and the first IL-6R inhibitor in the indication
([Roche](https://www.roche.com/media/releases/med-cor-2026-10-16)). **SatraGO-1
missed its week-24 primary endpoint**. **SatraGO-2** showed proptosis response
of 52.9% vs 23.4% and diplopia reduction of 60.7% vs 25.8%
([Ophthalmology Times](https://www.ophthalmologytimes.com/view/fda-approves-subcutaneous-enspryng-for-ted)).
Enspryng is the third approved TED drug, alongside Amgen's Tepezza and
Viridian's Lumvoa
([Healio](https://www.healio.com/news/ophthalmology/20261016/fda-approves-enspryng-for-thyroid-eye-disease)).

What follows:

- **Who it affects:**
  - **Bladder cancer:** IBRX, plus other BCG-unresponsive NMIBC developers
    (intravesical gene and drug-device programs).
  - **TED:** Amgen (Tepezza, IV infusion) and Viridian. An at-home subcutaneous
    option competes on convenience, not efficacy.
- **Read-through:** these approvals point the opposite way from last week's
  I-DXd withdrawal. The FDA will extrapolate across closely related disease
  biology, and will approve with one of two Phase 3 trials failing. It still
  rejects single-arm data where randomized trials are feasible. For valuation,
  separate "related-population extrapolation," which is now more likely, from
  "single-arm accelerated approval," which is now less likely.

## 3. BioNTech's BNT113 misses PFS

On **October 13**, **BioNTech** (NASDAQ: **BNTX**) said the Phase 2/3
**AHEAD-MERIT** trial missed its first dual primary endpoint. **BNT113**, an
mRNA immunotherapy encoding HPV16 E6/E7, plus pembrolizumab did not
significantly improve PFS over pembrolizumab alone in first-line HPV16+ head and
neck cancer. The independent monitoring committee recommended continuing to the
overall survival endpoint
([BioNTech](https://www.biontech.com/us/en/home/mediaroom/news/press-releases/2026/10/BioNTech-Provides-Update-on-Phase-2-3-Clinical-Trial-for-mRNA-Cancer-Immunotherapy-Candidate-BNT113-in-Metastatic-HPV16-positive-Head-and-Neck-Cancer.html)).
No hazard ratio was disclosed. Shares fell about 7%, and BMO now sees an OS win
as "less likely"
([BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/drug-development/biontech-faces-3rd-mrna-setback-as-head-and-neck-cancer-therapy-fails-to-improve-survival)).

- **Who it affects:** BNTX; off-the-shelf mRNA and viral-antigen cancer vaccines
  broadly. BioSpace counts this as BioNTech's third mRNA oncology setback.
- **Read-through:** when only one of two dual endpoints has been read, the
  question stays open. Until OS reads out, the program should be modeled as
  impaired, not as a binary event still ahead.

## 4. Women's health

- **Tukysa EU extension (Pfizer, PFE).** The CHMP recommended tucatinib with
  trastuzumab and pertuzumab as maintenance for HER2+ unresectable or metastatic
  breast cancer after first-line induction
  ([Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4653801-lilly-pfizer-jj-eu-drug-recommendations-this-month)).
  This follows the US approval on October 7. Lacuna: `acquirer-pfizer`, Breast
  Health.
- **TumorSight Risk (SimBioSys, private).** FDA 510(k) clearance for AI-derived
  5- and 10-year recurrence risk from routine breast MRI in HR+/HER2- stage
  I–IIIA disease
  ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/simbiosys-secures-fda-clearance-for-tumorsight-risk-introducing-the-worlds-first-ai-powered-mri-derived-breast-cancer-recurrence-risk-assessment-302910055.html)).
  - The clearance decision is dated September 25. The predicate device is
    ArteraAI Breast.
  - Validation was retrospective (n=2,129), so it shows that scores track past
    recurrence. It does not show that using the tool changes treatment or
    outcomes
    ([EconoTimes](https://econotimes.com/FDA-Clears-SimBioSys-Breast-Cancer-Recurrence-Risk-Software-1755126)).
  - The tool competes with genomic recurrence scores on speed and on needing no
    tissue. It has not shown equivalence for chemotherapy decisions.

## Also this week

- **Vaxcyte VAX-31 (PCVX), Oct 5, carried from last week.** OPUS-1 met all
  prespecified primary immunogenicity endpoints against PCV20/PCV21. The BLA is
  planned for H1 2028
  ([Vaxcyte](https://investors.vaxcyte.com/news-releases/news-release-details/vaxcyte-reports-positive-topline-data-opus-1-pivotal-adult-phase)).
  Against PCV21, serotypes 3 and 12F missed the >0.667 noninferiority bar in
  separate comparator analyses
  ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/vaxcyte-shares-rise-50-vax-121012786.html)).
- **Krystal KB113 (KRYS).** In a three-patient Gorlin syndrome pilot, 69 of 83
  basal cell carcinoma lesions regressed, including 40 of 47 non-injected
  lesions
  ([Stock Titan](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/KRYS/krystal-biotech-announces-positive-clinical-data-for-new-dermatology-qyunsn4wk2l6.html)).
  This is proof of concept only.
- **Elevar/HLB rivoceranib + camrelizumab.** The NDA was resubmitted on Oct 9
  after a third complete response letter, which cited cGMP deficiencies
  ([OncLive](https://www.onclive.com/view/elevar-resubmits-nda-for-rivoceranib-plus-camrelizumab-frontline-unresectable-metastatic-hcc)).
  The review clock has not been set.
- **Biosimilars.** The final postapproval manufacturing-changes guidance was
  published in the Federal Register on Oct 13. It aligns with ICH Q5E and
  extends the same principles to interchangeables
  ([RAPS](https://www.raps.org/resource/biosimilars-fda-finalizes-guidance-on-postapproval-manufacturing-changes.html)).
- **Peptide compounding.** On Oct 14, HHS and FDA sent an interim access policy
  for compounded peptides to OMB for review. It has not been issued
  ([Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/10/peptides-at-a-crossroads-what-wellness-providers-need-to-know)).
- **Excluded:** several outlets ran "FDA panel votes 10-0 against Xarelto in
  ACS" stories dated this week. They are 2012–2014 articles with new dates
  ([Reuters, 2014](https://www.reuters.com/article/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/us-fda-panel-rejects-jj-drug-for-acute-coronary-syndrome-idUSL2N0KQ25W/)).
  There was no new Xarelto event.
