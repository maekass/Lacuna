## Research Focus

My work addresses a fundamental challenge in translational medicine: the **asymmetric distribution of clinical trial intelligence** across stakeholder groups with divergent epistemological frameworks. I build production-grade ML systems that synthesize real-world evidence into actionable insights for quantitative investors, clinical researchers, and patient advocates—without privileging any single perspective or compromising methodological rigor.

**Current Research Questions:**
- Can ensemble ML models predict Phase 2/3 trial outcomes with >80% accuracy using only publicly available data?
- How do we construct multi-audience explanations that respect diverse cognitive frameworks without reductive simplification?
- What verification mechanisms establish institutional-grade trust in automated clinical intelligence systems?
